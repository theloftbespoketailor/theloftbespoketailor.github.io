A suit in Bangkok with only a day or two to spare

A suit can be made in Bangkok in twelve hours when the shop plans it with you in advance. Two or three days adds a second fitting. This guide sets out what each option gets you. It comes from The Loft Bespoke Tailor, a bespoke tailor inside BTS National Stadium station.

What can a tailor finish in twelve hours?

In twelve hours the shop runs the usual steps back to back, for a customer with a flight to catch.

Measuring comes first, in the morning, and the pattern is drafted for you straight away. The cloth is then cut and basted. After a fitting in the afternoon, the suit goes back to the bench and is done by the evening.

The express suit has one fitting, not two. Whether twelve hours is possible also depends on what is already on the bench, so tell the shop your dates first and ask. Stay within reach of the shop through the middle of the day.

What does a second fitting add?

A second fitting confirms that the corrections from the first one worked, and a suit that takes two to three days leaves time for it.

The first fitting is on a suit held together with basting stitches, so seams can still be moved. The second is on the finished suit, and you wear your own shoes to it.

When should you choose two or three days instead?

Choose the longer timeline whenever you can, since the second fitting exists to catch anything the first one left.

  • A wedding party should take it, because everyone in the party has to be right on the same day.
  • A first order from a new tailor should take it too. Two fittings let you judge the work before you collect it.

How do you book a suit before you land?

To book, message the shop on WhatsApp before you land, and give your flight time and your arrival date. The number is +66 85 048 7655.

  1. Arrive early on the day, with a rough idea of the cloth, so that measuring starts at once.
  2. Stay nearby for the afternoon fitting.

The shop can also measure you at your hotel in central Bangkok. Payment is taken in full at the order.
